# Discover SMEs

## Overview

Discover SMEs is a hyperlocal commerce platform built as a digital operating system for Festac Town, Lagos. It connects residents, Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs), and local government through trusted local business discovery, WhatsApp-native customer communication, intelligent booking flows, and community-level economic insights.

The platform is designed for:
- Local vendors who want better visibility, bookings, and customer management
- Customers who want trusted, convenient access to nearby products and services
- Local government agencies that want to support market formalization, compliance, and local economic growth

---

## Why Discover SMEs?

### For vendors
- Get discovered by nearby customers through category search and featured placements
- Accept booking requests and appointment confirmations directly from the platform
- Manage leads, inventory, and customer relationships in one dashboard
- Use WhatsApp and AI automation to respond faster and close more sales
- Build trust with verification badges and business profile credibility

### For customers
- Find trusted local businesses in Festac quickly
- Book services, compare vendors, and message businesses on WhatsApp instantly
- Track appointments, view business details, and make better buying decisions
- Use a mobile-friendly marketplace built for Nigerian commerce patterns

### For local government
- Promote digital formalization of Festac Town businesses
- Improve local economic visibility through vendor analytics and marketplace activity
- Support verified, compliant vendors with government-backed trust signals
- Enable community-focused business discovery and easier access to services

---

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. Hyperlocal discovery
Customers can search for businesses by category, location, verification level, and service type. The platform highlights nearby vendors, featured listings, and trusted service providers.

### 2. WhatsApp-native commerce
The platform is built around WhatsApp, Nigeria's dominant business messaging channel. Customers can click to chat, vendors can send automated reminders, and AI can respond to common inquiries.

### 3. Vendor operating system
Vendors get a modern dashboard for bookings, analytics, marketing, inventory, CRM, and payments. This turns small local shops into digitally managed businesses.

### 4. Civic & economic intelligence
Local government and civic stakeholders gain insight into the local economy through platform analytics, booking trends, vendor growth, and verification progress.

## Current Product Status

Discover SMEs is implemented as a full-stack platform with:
- Backend: Node.js, Express.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma
- Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- Shared code and types in a monorepo using workspaces
- WhatsApp Cloud API integration, AI commerce services, booking workflows, analytics dashboards, and vendor management tools

It is ready to be demonstrated as a working proof of concept for vendors, customers, and civic sponsors.

## Use Cases

### Vendor use case
A hair stylist in Festac lists their salon, publishes services, receives booking requests, and messages customers directly on WhatsApp. They track appointment confirmations, customer inquiries, and sales metrics from one dashboard.

### Customer use case
A resident searches for nearby electricians, compares verified vendors, books a service appointment, and confirms availability through WhatsApp in less than five minutes.

### Local government use case
A local government officer monitors vendor verification status, emerging service categories, and transaction activity to identify growth sectors and plan targeted support.

## Why this matters for Festac Town

Festac Town is home to thousands of informal businesses. Discover SMEs helps these businesses move from phone-based, manual operations into a digital ecosystem that is:
- mobile-first and WhatsApp-friendly
- trust-driven and easy to access
- supportive of micro-SME growth
- connected to local civic infrastructure

By making local commerce discoverable and manageable, Discover SMEs can help raise incomes, reduce friction, and strengthen Festac’s local marketplace.

## Contact & Deployment Notes

The project can be deployed using Render and integrates with:

- Meta WhatsApp Cloud API for messaging
- OpenAI for AI response automation
- Paystack or similar payment gateway for vendor payments and subscriptions

For a full technical handoff, the app includes deployment scripts, database migrations, seed data, and a development workflow documented in the project README.
